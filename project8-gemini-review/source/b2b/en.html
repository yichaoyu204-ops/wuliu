Input format and matching rules

The first nonempty spreadsheet row must contain Supplier, Shipment, Charge, Currency, Unit and Amount. Chinese headers are also supported. Use an agreed total for that shipment and charge. A customer selling price or a rate per unit needs separate procurement evidence and quantities.

The reviewer checks supplier, shipment, charge, currency and billing unit. It recognizes equivalent labels such as kg and 公斤. Different units or currencies require review. Supported currencies are CNY, USD, EUR, GBP, HKD, AUD, CAD, SGD, NZD and CHF, with up to two decimals. Tax, FX conversion, tiered rates and split allocations require separate calculations.

Review every text PDF candidate against the original. For scans or irregular tables, add a line with its page or row and an evidence note. Export before refreshing or closing the page. Use HTTPS or open the local server.
